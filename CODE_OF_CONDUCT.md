# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, and publication of another person's private information are
not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces and to public interactions representing
the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the
contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

Project maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when
this policy is violated.
